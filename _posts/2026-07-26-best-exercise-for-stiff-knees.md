The Best Exercise for Stiff Knees: Walking + Gentle Low‑Impact Movement  
Walking is the single most effective exercise for stiff knees because it:

- increases blood flow  
- warms the joint  
- reduces inflammation  
- strengthens supporting muscles  
- improves lubrication inside the knee  

Even 5 minutes can make a noticeable difference.

1-morning-walk.webp

Low‑impact alternatives include:

- cycling  
- swimming  
- elliptical  
- slow treadmill walking  

These movements reduce stiffness without stressing the joint.

🧘 1. Straight‑Leg Raises  
Straight‑leg raises strengthen the quadriceps — the main stabilizing muscle for your knees.

**How to do it:**

- Lie on your back.  
- Bend one knee, keep the other straight.  
- Tighten the thigh of the straight leg.  
- Lift it slowly.  
- Lower with control.  

**Reps:** 10–12 per leg

🧍 2. Mini Squats  
Mini squats build strength without deep bending.

**How to do it:**

- Stand with feet shoulder‑width apart.  
- Bend your knees slightly.  
- Keep your chest lifted.  
- Return to standing.  

**Reps:** 10–12

Mini squats improve mobility and help reduce stiffness over time.

🦶 3. Calf Raises  
Calf strength supports both the ankle and knee.

**How to do it:**

- Stand tall.  
- Lift your heels slowly.  
- Lower with control.  

**Reps:** 12–15

This exercise improves balance and reduces pressure on the knee joint.

🧘 4. Hamstring Stretch  
Tight hamstrings pull on the knee joint and increase stiffness.

**How to do it:**

- Sit with one leg straight.  
- Reach toward your toes.  
- Hold for 15–20 seconds.

🧘 5. Quad Stretch  
Loosening the front of the thigh reduces pressure on the knee.

**How to do it:**

- Stand and hold a wall for balance.  
- Bend one knee.  
- Hold your foot behind you.  
- Keep knees close together.

This stretch helps reduce stiffness after long periods of sitting.

🦵 6. Seated Knee Extensions  
This gentle movement improves mobility and reduces stiffness.

**How to do it:**

- Sit in a chair.  
- Straighten one leg.  
- Lower slowly.  

**Reps:** 10–12

Great for beginners or anyone with limited mobility.

🧘 7. Glute Bridges  
Strong glutes reduce knee strain.

**How to do it:**

- Lie on your back.  
- Lift your hips.  
- Squeeze glutes.  
- Lower slowly.  

**Reps:** 10–15

This exercise supports knee alignment and stability.

🧠 Tips for Reducing Knee Stiffness

- Move every 30–45 minutes.  
- Warm up before exercise.  
- Use heat in the morning.  
- Strengthen supporting muscles.  
- Add anti‑inflammatory foods to your diet.
