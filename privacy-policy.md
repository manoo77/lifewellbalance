## Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect on the site includes:

### Personal Data

Personally identifiable information, such as your name, shipping address, email address, and telephone number, and demographic information, such as your age, gender, hometown, and interests, that you voluntarily give to us when you:

- Register for access to certain portions of our site
- Subscribe to our newsletter
- Participate in contests, sweepstakes, or surveys
- Make a purchase
- Use interactive areas of our site (message boards, chat rooms, etc.)

### Derivative Data

Information our servers automatically collect when you access the site, such as:

- Your IP address
- Your browser type and version
- Your operating system
- Your Internet Service Provider
- The pages you view on our site
- The dates and times of your visits
- Referring website addresses

### Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies, web beacons, tracking pixels, and other tracking technologies to help customize the site and improve your experience. Cookies are small data files stored on your device that:

- Remember your username and password (sparing you from re-entering them)
- Control how often you see particular advertisements
- Help us understand which pages are most popular

**You can control cookies:** Most web browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete them. However, if you disable cookies, some features of our site may not function properly.
